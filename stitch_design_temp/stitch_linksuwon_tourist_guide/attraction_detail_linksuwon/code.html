About

Hwaseong Haenggung is a temporary palace where King Jeongjo and the royal family resided while traveling or during times of war. Constructed in 1789, it stands as the largest and most beautiful 'Haenggung' in Korea. It served as the center of local administration and was the site of grand royal banquets. Today, visitors can explore the faithfully reconstructed halls and witness traditional guard changing ceremonies, offering a deep dive into Joseon Dynasty history.
