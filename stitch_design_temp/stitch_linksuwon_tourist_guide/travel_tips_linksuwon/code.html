Travel Tips

Essential information for a smooth journey in Suwon.

directions_bus

Transportation

Use a T-money card for buses and subways. Top up at convenience stores. Suwon Station is the main hub.

Taxi App

Kakao T is highly recommended for hailing taxis. You can pay cash to the driver if you don't link a card.

Payment

Cards are widely accepted, but keep some cash (KRW) for traditional markets like Paldalmun Market.

Emergency

Dial 112 for Police and 119 for Fire/Ambulance. English translation services are usually available.
