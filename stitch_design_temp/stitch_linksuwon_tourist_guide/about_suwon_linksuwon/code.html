King Jeongjo's Vision, 1794

A Fortress Built on Filial Piety

Constructed in the late 18th century by King Jeongjo to honor his father, Suwon Hwaseong Fortress stands as a masterpiece of defensive military architecture mixed with profound aesthetic beauty.

Unlike traditional fortresses, it was designed with advanced scientific principles of the era, utilizing both brick and stone. Today, its 5.7 kilometers of walls embrace the city center, offering a living museum that breathes life into the modern streets.
